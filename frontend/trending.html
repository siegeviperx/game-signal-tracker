Methodology and limits

Each observed metric is source-specific. Steam samples measure players online at a particular time, not daily peak CCU. Twitch measures viewers in up to 100 streams, not all Twitch activity. YouTube counters are cumulative per video, not unique viewers or game sales. Editorial coverage counts only reviewed game links.

When sufficient data exist, within-source movement is compared with the prior seven-day period and ranked against games in the same primary genre and broad platform cohort. Independent source percentiles are combined with provisional weights and a coverage penalty. Sparse or incomparable sources withhold the index entirely. Model probabilities remain unavailable pending historical training and out-of-time backtesting.
